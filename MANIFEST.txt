Fixes included in this version (on top of the first delivery):
  - search.tsx was missing <StoreHeader />/<StoreFooter /> entirely (every
    route renders these itself — there's no shared layout route) — that's
    why the top bar was gone on /search.
  - Category/brand filters now round-trip through the URL as native arrays
    instead of a comma-joined string, and the parser accepts either shape.
  - The price slider in SearchFilters.tsx is now clamped + force-remounted
    when the catalog's price bounds shift (e.g. after picking a category
    or toggling in-stock-only), removing a real edge case where it could
    briefly receive an out-of-range value on any filter change.
